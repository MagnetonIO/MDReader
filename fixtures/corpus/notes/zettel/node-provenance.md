# Provenance

Provenance records where each fact came from: the source file, the byte range,
and the ingest run. It is what lets an answer cite itself.

[[node-ingest]] attaches it on the way in; [[node-query-engine]] surfaces it on
the way out. Without it, [[node-search]] results would be unauditable.
