# Typical Frontmatter

A normal note with a full YAML frontmatter block: strings, a date, a list of
tags, a boolean, and a number. The body then continues as usual.

This is the happy path for the frontmatter parser. Compare with the
[empty](empty-frontmatter.md), [malformed](malformed-frontmatter.md), and
[frontmatter-only](frontmatter-only.md) cases.
